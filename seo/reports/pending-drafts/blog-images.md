# Blog images — free sources, prompts, alt text, placement

No paid credits needed. Two free routes per image:
- **Stock (fastest, free):** open a search link, download a royalty-free
  photo (Pexels/Unsplash are free for commercial use, no attribution
  required — but a credit is polite).
- **Generate free:** paste the prompt into any free image generator
  (Google Gemini / "nano banana", Bing Image Creator, etc.).

Upload the chosen image in wp-admin on the matching draft, set it as the
**Featured image**, and paste the **alt text** below.

## Tips
- Aim for landscape ~1200x675 (16:9); keep files under ~200KB (WP Rocket /
  an image optimizer will compress on upload).
- Prefer product/texture shots over stocky model photos — they read as more
  authentic for the brand and won't clash with your real product pages.
- Best of all: your own real product/install photos. Swap these in whenever
  you have them.
