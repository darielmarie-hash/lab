## Findings

### 1. No LocalBusiness schema (blocks "hair extensions Chicago" + local AI)
Homepage carries `Organization` + `WebSite` schema but **not**
`LocalBusiness`/`Store`, and no address or telephone in structured data.
AI engines answering "where to buy hair extensions in Chicago" or "Black-
owned hair vendors near me" lean on LocalBusiness + NAP. **Fix:** add
LocalBusiness/Store schema with name, Chicago address, phone, hours, geo,
and `sameAs` social links (Local agent + On-Page).

### 2. Quotable Q&A is thin outside product pages
Only product pages carry FAQPage schema (Capri Silk yes; Edge Entity no).
Category pages and the About page have **no Q&A blocks**. AI engines lift
concise question→answer pairs. **Fix:** add 3–5 FAQ Q&As (with FAQPage
schema) to each money category page and key products, phrased as the exact
questions buyers ask ("Is Capri Silk real human hair?", "How many bundles
for a full sew-in?", "Do tape-ins damage 4c edges?").

### 3. Entity clarity — About page exists but under-leveraged
`/about/` exists (good). Confirm it states plainly, in the first paragraph:
what Cashé's is, who it serves (retail + salon pros), since when (2018),
and where (Chicago). Add Organization `founder`, `foundingDate`,
`areaServed`. This is what makes an engine confident enough to name you.

### 4. Proof/reviews not surfaced as structured data sitewide
Site has a reviews plugin and product AggregateRating (Edge Entity shows
one). Ensure Review/AggregateRating renders on all products and that
testimonials appear on category/About pages — review density feeds both
rankings and AI trust.

Record for each: is Cashé's cited? who is? which sources do they cite?
Baseline expectation today: **not cited** (the litmus test from the video).
Re-test 14–21 days after the fixes ship.
