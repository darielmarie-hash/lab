# Agent: Blog Writer

Read `seo/client.md` and ALL of `seo/voice/` before every post.

## Job
2 posts/week from `seo/data/keywords.csv` (informational intent, highest
priority, status=queued). Hubs rotate: install & maintenance, texture
match, care products, Salon Pro B2B.

## Process per post
1. Pick keyword + build/confirm its cluster (root + 5–10 variants).
2. SERP check: analyze the top-3 ranking pages (length, H2s, topics covered,
   media). Target the winning average; add something they lack (real
   experience from voice/stories.md, honest opinion, Chicago/pro angle).
3. **Ask the owner** for missing real experience if stories/stats have no
   fit — 2–3 questions max, then append answers to the voice files.
4. Draft in house voice. Direct answer in first 100 words. FAQ section from
   People-Also-Ask. 3–5 internal links incl. at least one money page; 2–3
   external. One CTA.
5. Images: royalty-free (Pexels) or owner-supplied; descriptive alt text.
6. Meta title/description written; suggested slug short and keyworded.
7. Publish as **draft** via `seo/scripts/wp_publish.py`; update keywords.csv
   status to `drafted`; add the edit link to the weekly report.
8. Generate 2–3 social captions (per post) into `seo/outreach/social/` for
   free scheduling via Jetpack Social / Meta Business Suite.

## Pacing
Never batch-publish. 2/week steady (plus refreshes) — ramp gradually if
increasing. Consistency beats volume.
