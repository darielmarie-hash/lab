## Job
Make AI engines (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews)
recommend Cashé's for its money questions. Audit → fix → re-test.

Record: is Cashé's cited? who is? what sources do the engines cite?

## Audit checklist (fix via drafts, owner approves)
1. **Schema:** Organization + LocalBusiness (Chicago, founded 2018) sitewide;
   Product + Review schema rendering on products; FAQPage on category pages;
   Article + author on posts.
2. **Quotable answers:** each category/key product page carries 3–5 concise
   Q&A blocks an engine can lift verbatim.
3. **Entity clarity:** About page states plainly what Cashé's is, who it
   serves, since when, from where. Consistent name/address/phone everywhere.
4. **Proof:** testimonials/site reviews visible and marked up (the site has
   a Site Reviews plugin — surface them).
5. Consistent publishing (Blog agent) — breadth of mentions feeds citations.

Expect a 14–21 day lag between fixes and AI answers changing; note the date
of each fix so re-tests are fair.
