# Voice & Style — Cashé's Hair Emporium

The writing standard for every post and page. Model the three 2026 posts:
- "How to Keep a Sew In From Tangling and Matting"
- "Scalp Care Under a Wig, Sew-In or Extensions: The Guide Nobody Gave You"
- "The Coily-Girl's Texture-Match Guide"

## Rules
1. **Talk to one reader**, second person, like a trusted stylist friend —
   warm, direct, a little playful. Never corporate, never "in today's
   fast-paced world" filler.
2. **Be specific or be silent.** Name textures (3c, 4a, 4b, 4c), methods
   (sew-in, tape-in, i-tip, k-tip), products, timeframes, and prices where
   true. Generic advice is deleted on review.
3. **Open with the reader's problem** in the first two sentences — the itch,
   the shedding, the tangle at 11pm — not with a definition of hair.
4. **Answer-first formatting:** the direct answer within the first 100 words
   (good for readers, featured snippets, and AI citation), then the depth.
5. **Every post links** to 3–5 relevant Cashé's pages (products, categories,
   related posts) and 2–3 trusted external sources. One clear CTA.
6. **Honesty sells:** say when a cheaper fix works, when to see a stylist,
   what a product won't do. It's why readers trust the recommendation.
7. Keep paragraphs short. Use H2/H3 questions people actually search.

## Banned
Filler intros, "unlock/elevate/journey" cliches, keyword stuffing, fake
urgency, claims we can't back, and any paragraph that could appear on a
competitor's blog unchanged.
