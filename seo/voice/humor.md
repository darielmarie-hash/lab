# Humor Register

We're the stylist friend who keeps it real, not a comedy act. Humor shows
up as:
- Self-aware nods to real moments: the 11pm detangling session, the bonnet
  economy, "we've all slept on wet braids and paid for it."
- Gentle exaggeration about relatable pain (matting, shedding, itchy
  sew-in week two) — never about the reader.
- One wink per section at most. If a joke competes with the answer, cut it.
- Never punch down, never mock budgets, textures, or skill levels.

Test: would a client laugh AND still trust us with a $300 bundle order?
Both or rewrite.
